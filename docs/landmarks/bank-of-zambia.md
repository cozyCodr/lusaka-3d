# Bank of Zambia — reference notes

Head office off Cairo Road, north of Independence Avenue. Model:
`src/landmarks/bank-of-zambia.js`.

## Sources

- **Founder's reference photos (2026-09-27)** — eight images. Six are the
  Lusaka head office (front elevations by day and dusk, a view with the
  skybridges, a corner view, and a view from an upper floor over the fountain
  lawn). Two are **not** this building and were not used: one is labelled
  "Ndola the Friendly City" (the Ndola regional office, stepped terraced
  floors), and one shows the same stepped design.
- **OpenStreetMap** — way 283005464 (`building=commercial`, 8 levels,
  "Bank of Zambia"): a 54.3 × 28.4 m rectangle, long sides at 350° / 170°.
  The 8-level block 16 m to the south (way 283005457, unnamed in OSM) is
  the Bank's south block and the skybridge partner (founder, 2026-09-27). Cairo Road ~80 m west, Sapele Avenue ~18 m east, a service road
  ~35 m west of the front.
- **Esri World Imagery, zoom 19** — roof with solar panels and a skylight;
  the bridge link to the south block; fenced parking and carports on the
  west, a car park on the east by the railway.

## Known gaps

- The fin rhythm is uniform; the real facade pairs windows between wider piers.
- The rear (east) facade and roof plant are inferred.
- The south block is built from the edges of the head-office photos; its top
  floor's chamfered corner and street-level shopfronts are simplified.
