# Manda Hill Mall — reference notes

Zambia's first shopping mall (1999), on Great East Road in Northmead,
redeveloped and extended in 2013–16. Model: `src/landmarks/manda-hill.js`.

## Sources

- **OpenStreetMap** — the mall outline, way 288298886, including four
  entrance blocks projecting from the front. The tiler skips it.
- **Esri World Imagery, zoom 18–19** — the roof layout, the bright atrium
  roof in the middle, the two parking decks in front with the entrance drive
  between them and a ramp opening in the south-west deck, and the palms along
  the road.
- **Web photos (reference only)** — a drone view from a YouTube tour (2022),
  front and entrance photos on Alamy and iStock (2017–2021), Hyprop's 2016
  annual report, Regent Lighting's project page, Lusaka Times (2019) and
  Moneyweb (2019). Pre-2013 photos show the old mall and were not used.

## Measurements

## Known gaps

- Shop signs are generic colour blocks, not the real brands.
- The back and sides are plain walls; the service yards and loading bays are not modelled.
- The curved ramp from the road at the south-west end and the lower car park interior are simplified.
- No founder's photos yet: the entrance blocks and the canopy details are the least certain parts.
