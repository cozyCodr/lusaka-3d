# Cathedral of the Holy Cross — reference notes

Anglican cathedral at 74 Independence Avenue, beside the roundabout east of
the Cabinet Office. Model: `src/landmarks/cathedral.js`.

## Sources

- **Founder's reference photos (2026-09-27)** — nine views: an aerial from
  the south-east, three of the entrance front, two three-quarter views, the
  south side (peaked glass bays over gabled aisles), the north side (brick
  with slit windows, white grille bay, annex), and a lawn view with palms.
- **OpenStreetMap** — way 1422882767 (`building=cathedral`, anglican): a
  cross-shaped outline ~92 m long on a 82° / 262° axis, with a pointed east
  end (the entrance prow), side bulges near the front, and a narrower
  pointed west end.
- **Esri World Imagery, zoom 19** — the square pitched canopy at the east
  end confirms the entrance faces east; folded-plate roof ribs run across
  the nave; the sawtooth aisle shows on the south side.

## Known gaps

- The folds in the nave walls are shown by ribs and peaked tops; the walls
  themselves are flat, not pleated in plan.
- The aisle gables are simple pentagons; the photos show diagonal brick folds.
- The north side and west end are approximated from one photo and the outline.
