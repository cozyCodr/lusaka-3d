# National Heroes Stadium and Gabon Disaster Memorial — reference notes

West of Great North Road, north of the city centre. Models:
`src/landmarks/heroes-stadium.js` (both).

## Sources

- **Founder's reference photos (2026-09-27)** — 16 images: two aerials, the
  main front head-on (glass curtain wall between orange towers), the petal
  roof from below and above, the bowl interior (seats, track, scoreboard),
  side views with louvres and ramps, "Gate No 4", an OSM map extract, and the
  Gabon Disaster Memorial with the stadium behind it. Several are
  watermarked stock images, used only as reference.
- **OpenStreetMap** — relation 9099651 (`building=stadium`, "National
  Heroes Stadium"): outer ring 262 × 306 m, inner ring (roof opening)
  179 × 219 m, long axis north–south; pitch way 379231384 (67 × 109 m);
  Gabon Disaster Memorial node at -15.368945, 28.268884. Great North Road
  ~370 m east. The map was widened north to -15.36 and west to 28.262 to
  include them.

## Known gaps

- Petals are identical half-ellipsoids; the real ones taper where they meet.
- Seat colours are simplified (the real bowl has green lettering in the blue).
- Ramps and gates are indicative; the second, smaller stadium to the
  south-west is only the plain OSM shape.
- The memorial's grave layout is not surveyed.
