# Indo Zambia Bank head office — reference notes

Indo House, Plot 1213–1214, corner of Great East Road and Addis Ababa Drive,
Rhodespark: the bank's head office, a long building along Great East Road
just south-west of Manda Hill. Model: `src/landmarks/indo-zambia-bank.js`.

## Sources

- **Founder's reference photos (2026-09-29)** — six views: the street
  elevation along Great East Road (three), the entrance and forecourt at the
  south-west end, the north-east end wall from the junction with the
  footbridge, and the rear.
- **Address** — Indo-Zambia Bank Limited (Wikipedia; Bloomberg LEI record).
- **Esri World Imagery, zoom 19** — the plan, the stepped roofs with solar
  panels, and the forecourt; zoom 20 is not available here.
- **OpenStreetMap** — way 1061966800 (a Microsoft footprint traced during
  construction); the tiler skips it.

## Known gaps

- Floor heights and depths are estimated from the photos (3.6 m a floor).
- The rear and the back of the site are simplified to a plain office block.
- The logo is drawn, not traced.
