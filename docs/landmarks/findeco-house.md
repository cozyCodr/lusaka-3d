# Findeco House — reference notes

Zambia's tallest building, at the junction of Cairo Road and Independence
Avenue. Model: `src/landmarks/findeco.js`.

## Sources

- **Founder's reference photos (2026-09-26)** — six views across the decades:
  Samsung-era close elevation, a 1980s street view, a telephoto elevation, a
  crown close-up, a black-and-white Cairo Road shot, and a Tecno-era view from
  the Independence Ave junction. The v2 model is built from these.
- **Wikipedia, FINDECO House** — 90 m (295 ft), 23 floors, built 1978–79,
  Yugoslav modernism, architects Dušan Milenković & Branimir Ganović, main
  contractor ZECCO; rooftop sign since 2013 (Samsung, later Tecno and others).
- **OpenStreetMap** — way 1069663212 (`building=office`, wikidata Q21007716):
  the podium footprint, 52.8 × 34 m with notches, edges at bearings 80° / 350°.
- **Esri World Imagery, zoom 19** (0.288 m/px, measured at a known scale):
  roof including the flared parapet ≈ 33 m across.

## Known gaps

- Crown signs are plain panels: no brand artwork, and the real sign changes
  every few years (Samsung, Tecno, now green).
- Podium detail (shopfronts, canopies along Cairo Road) is simplified.
- Corbel and bracket counts are read from photos, not drawings.
