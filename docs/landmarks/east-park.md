# East Park Mall — reference notes

EastPark Mall at Great East Road and Thabo Mbeki Road: the main mall, the
newer retail strips to the south and Builders Warehouse to the north-east.
Model: `src/landmarks/east-park.js`.

## Sources

- **Founder's reference photos (2026-09-29)** — twenty views: the mall
  directory map; the dark west facade with its shop signs, SHOPRITE letters
  and the Galaxy Casino sign; the Keg's louvred drum and its court with the
  fountain, tensile tents and cacti; the stone-pillar entrances (Bymart
  "3", Food Lover's Market); the covered walkways; the Shoprite Select wing
  with its stone sign pillar; Pick n Pay and Edgars; the charcoal south
  strips (Micmar, Sikale, the ATM block); the amphitheatre.
- **Google Maps places (2026)** — positions of Galaxy Casino (west end),
  Pick n Pay (south-west), Shoprite and Shoprite Select (north-east wing),
  RocoMamas, Gourmet, Micmar (south strip, by the roundabout), KFC.
- **OpenStreetMap** — ways 614461977 (mall), 437561941 (Builders
  Warehouse), 802797488 and 802797489 (south strips), and the car parks
  681628960, 802797491, 802797484. The tiler skips the four buildings.
- **Esri World Imagery, zoom 18** — the plan and the car parks.

## Known gaps

- Entrance positions and numbers are placed by rule, not surveyed; the directory map was too small to read.
- Individual shop signs are generic apart from Shoprite, Galaxy Casino, Hungry Lion, Doğtaş, Pick n Pay, Edgars, Keg, Micmar, Sikale and Builders.
- The two-storey units in the south strips, the ATM block and the car dealers are not modelled separately.
- The Xerox / Haval building in one photo could not be placed.
