# Hilton Garden Inn, Society Business Park — reference notes

Hotel tower in the Society Business Park, just west of Cairo Road. Model:
`src/landmarks/hilton-garden-inn.js`.

## Sources

- **Web photos (2026-09-27)** — Google Images results from Hilton,
  Tripadvisor, Agoda and Travel Weekly: views from the south along Cairo
  Road, from the north-west, a street view past the old red-brick Society
  House, the entrance canopy, and night views. Used only as reference.
- **Hilton / listing sites** — 18 hotel floors, 148 rooms.
- **OpenStreetMap** — way 1185077883 (`building=hotel`), 25 × 18 m, long
  axis at bearing 80°; business-park buildings around it (ways 1311041465–67).
