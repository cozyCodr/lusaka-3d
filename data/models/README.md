## `corolla.glb` — the drivable car

**"Toyota Corolla 2020"** by **ItsDiyor**, licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/toyota-corolla-2020-6d7d34ee42734d1ab28a6b1f1c5fc4fc

Changes made for this project (with [`tools/prepare_car.mjs`](../../tools/prepare_car.mjs)):
the four wheels split out of the merged body meshes with pivots at their
hubs, the 0.1 scale baked in, textures resized to 1024 px WebP, and positions
quantized. In the app the paint gets a clear coat, the lamp lenses glow, and
the number plates are replaced with Zambian-style plates that carry this credit.
